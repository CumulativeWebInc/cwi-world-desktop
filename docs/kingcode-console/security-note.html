🛡 KingCode Console — honest security note

Read this once. It says what the console protects, and what it does not.

What stays on your laptop

  • Your voice: recognition (Vosk) runs on this machine. Audio is never sent anywhere for speech recognition.
  • Your voiceprint embedding: stored only here, encrypted with AES-256 (key in your OS keychain).
  • Your PIN hash, authorization audit log, and ping inbox: same — local and encrypted.

What leaves your laptop

  • Your words to KingCode through the chat tab leave the machine — that's how I (the agent) receive them, same as chatting in a browser. The chat tab is a window into the normal Muse chat, with its normal privacy.
  • Anything you ask me to publish, send, or look up goes out by definition — that's the job, and sensitive ones always ask for your tap first.

The recording attack — stated plainly

A recording of your voice can fool the voiceprint. This is true of every consumer voice-verification system, including this one. That is exactly why:

  • Voice alone NEVER authorizes sensitive actions. Publishing, credentials, logins, money, wallet signing, external sends — these always require your in-app tap (plus PIN if you set one). This is enforced in code, not policy.
  • Voiceprint unlocks routine commands only (status checks, navigation, questions).
  • A stolen recording can at most make the console talk — it cannot make it spend, sign, or publish.
  • Challenge-response (Security tab) raises the bar for elevated moments: you repeat random digits, so a fixed recording won't pass.

Privacy tiers & presenter mode

  • Everything the world renders is tiered PUBLIC / PRIVATE / SECRET. New content types default to PRIVATE.
  • PRIVATE/SECRET render as ••• until your voice verifies or you tap/PIN.
  • Presenter mode (🎥 button, or Ctrl+Shift+P) instantly hides every non-public panel and enables OS-level capture protection. Use it for screen shares, demos, streams, or anyone looking over your shoulder.
  • OS screenshots taken outside the app are outside the app's control — presenter mode is the mitigation. Turn it on before you share your screen.

Your controls

  • Wake word can be toggled off — the mic button still works.
  • Delete your voiceprint any time (Security tab) — one tap, gone from the laptop.
  • The authorization audit log shows every allow/deny decision the console made.
